## Title IX Office / where to report
- **Office for the Prevention of Harassment & Discrimination (OPHD)** houses the campus
  **Title IX Officer** and receives reports of sexual violence and sexual harassment.
  Report via the OPHD website, email, or phone: https://ophd.berkeley.edu
- Reports are governed by the **UC systemwide SVSH Policy**, which applies across all UC
  campuses.

## Confidential support (does not trigger a report)
- **PATH to Care Center** — confidential survivor advocacy, available to help with options,
  accommodations, and accompaniment. 24/7 **Care Line: (510) 643-2005** *(verify)*.
  https://care.berkeley.edu
- Confidential advocates are distinct from OPHD/Title IX; speaking with them does not by
  itself start a University investigation.

## Immediate safety / emergency
- Immediate danger: **911**.
- **UC Police Department (UCPD) Berkeley:** (510) 642-3333 *(verify)*.

## California framework (affirmative consent, SB 967)
- **Affirmative consent:** affirmative, conscious, voluntary agreement; ongoing and
  revocable; silence/lack of resistance is not consent; a person who is incapacitated
  (asleep, unconscious, or impaired) cannot consent.
- **Preponderance of the evidence** standard.
- California bars informal resolution/mediation for sexual-violence cases
  (Cal. Education Code § 66262.5).

## Process (follows the federal Title IX baseline; see title-ix-federal.md)
Reporting → intake & supportive measures (with or without a formal complaint) → (if a formal
complaint) notice to respondent → investigation by trained personnel → evidence review →
hearing with advisor cross-examination → written determination → appeal. An advisor of
choice (attorney or non-attorney) is permitted; the UC process provides one for the hearing
if needed.

## Supportive measures & no-contact orders
Academic, housing, work, and schedule accommodations; mutual no-contact directives; escort
services; referrals to PATH to Care — available regardless of whether a formal complaint is
filed. (Confirm the current menu and request process with OPHD/PATH to Care.)

## External options
U.S. Department of Education Office for Civil Rights (OCR); California Civil Rights
Department (CRD); local law enforcement. A civil lawsuit is a separate option with its own
deadlines — consult an attorney.
